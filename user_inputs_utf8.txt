# FOLKReach — Relationship Management & Follow-up Platform

Build a complete, production-quality, modern web application called **FOLKReach**.

The application is designed for outreach teams who meet many new people through reaching, book distribution, college outreach, events, sessions, personal meetings, calls, and other activities.

**“Meet people. Remember people. Understand people. Follow up at the right time. Never lose a relationship.”**

FOLKReach must therefore act as an intelligent relationship-management assistant that continuously tells the user:

* Who should I contact today?

* Who has an upcoming follow-up?
* Who has been neglected?
* Who should I meet?
* What did I discuss with this person previously?
* Which topics have already been discussed?
* Which topics have never been discussed?
* Was Prasadam given recently?
* What happened during the last call?
* What happened during the last one-to-one?
* What should I do next?
